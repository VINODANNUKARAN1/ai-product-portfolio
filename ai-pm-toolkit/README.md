# 🧠 AI Product Manager Toolkit

## Open-Source Frameworks, Templates & Decision Tools for AI PMs

A collection of practical tools I use daily as an AI Product Manager building intelligent products at scale across APAC.

## Why This Exists
Most AI PM resources are either too theoretical (academic papers) or too code-heavy (ML tutorials). This fills the gap: **strategic product thinking applied specifically to AI/ML products.**
